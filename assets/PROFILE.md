- `vigil-avatar.png`: purple glitch-font V, black background, circular-crop safe.

## Avatar prompt (verbatim)

Create a square bot profile picture. Use the attached VIGIL lettering ONLY as the font/style reference. The new image must show EXACTLY ONE uppercase letter: "V". No other letters. Match the reference's chunky angular pixel/terminal hacker typography, sharp rectangular steps, purple layered offset outline echoes, and horizontal glitch-band accents. A bold unmistakable V shape with deep purple upper portion, electric violet middle and bright lilac lower portion; flat graphic pixel art, crisp hard edges, restrained offset wireframe shadows. Center the single V on a completely plain pure black background. Letter occupies around 60 percent of width and 60 percent of height, all outline echoes contained within circular-avatar safe margins. NO eye, no face, no shield, no metallic 3D surface, no gradients in the background, no extra words, no icons, no watermark. This is a clean finished standalone avatar, not a mockup.

Reference: the user's supplied purple VIGIL font image.

## Banner prompt (verbatim)

Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished ultra-wide profile banner for VIGIL. Canvas must be 3:1 landscape, three times wider than tall, designed like a 1500x500 header. Match the attached lettering and V avatar: chunky sharp angular pixel/terminal hacker font, flat graphic style, layered thin offset purple outline echoes and subtle broken horizontal glitch strips. Exact text is "VIGIL", V-I-G-I-L, one word, spelled correctly, centered and highly legible. Letters have dark purple upper sections, electric violet middle bands and brighter lilac lower bands, matching the avatar. Plain pure black background with spacious margins; wordmark occupies about 65% of canvas width and middle 55% of height. Keep all important details inside central crop-safe area. No eye, no face, no shield, no extra symbols, no 3D metal, no slogans, no watermark, no UI mockup. Show just this clean graphic banner edge to edge.

References: the user's purple VIGIL font image and the selected V avatar.
